Data quality, kept visible

    Review cases are excluded from delayed and on-time totals. An undated order cannot enter a due-date cohort.

    What does this report mean?

    Delayed means completed after its due time, or still without a usable completion before the cutoff. Needs review means an undated completion or conflicting copies of the same source event prevent a timing conclusion.

    Exact duplicate events are retained in the source and collapsed before aggregation. A completion exactly at the end cutoff belongs to the next snapshot. The earliest valid completion is used; reopened orders, partial quantities, shifts and working-day calendars are outside this prototype.

    This is an event-time view over the current dataset. It cannot reconstruct what an operator knew historically because the fixture has no ingestion-time history. Checksums identify data; they do not authenticate it.

    Why the join fails

    One order has several planned steps. It can also have several raw event rows. Joining both directly multiplies those rows, then COUNT(*) counts the result.

    The corrected query groups events and steps before joining one row per order. A replay and a conflicting event are different problems, handled separately.
